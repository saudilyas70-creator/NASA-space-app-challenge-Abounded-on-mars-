# Mission photo archive

The galleries use locally stored NASA/JPL archive images. The photo groups follow the visible subject and the source archive caption. Captions and alternative text describe each image; artist concepts are identified as such. The images are bundled under `images/missions/`, so the site does not need to fetch them from a remote server.
